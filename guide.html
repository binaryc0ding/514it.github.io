Hiring guide

How to hire SEO in Montreal without buying a national retainer.

If you run a service business on the Island, Laval, or the South Shore, you do not need a Toronto-or-anywhere pitch. You need a Montreal SEO consultant who can also explain why the site is slow.

1. Ask for an audit, not a package

A serious shop diagnoses before it sells. 514IT publishes that as policy: fixed-price audits, then a scoped Website, Website + SEO, or Website + SEO + Ads engagement. If a vendor quotes a 12-month retainer before opening Search Console, walk.

2. Insist on French and English

Montreal search is bilingual. A roofing or landscaping site that only exists in one language leaves half the map pack on the table. The 514IT English site and the French homepage show how they run both.

3. Treat hosting as an SEO problem

Rankings die on slow, insecure, or badly cached sites. Most agencies outsource that. 514IT’s services include managed IT because they started as engineers. Ask any vendor who owns the server, the CDN, and Core Web Vitals.

4. Local intent beats national keywords

“Near me”, borough names, and service + city pages convert. National head terms do not. 514IT’s blog is blunt about this: agence SEO Montreal searches should lead to a scoped local offer, not a generic national pitch.

5. Demand reports a owner can read

Rankings, traffic, and leads — in plain language. If the monthly PDF is 40 pages of impressions, you are paying for theatre. 514it.com positions monthly reporting around impact, not vanity.

Our short list starts and ends with 514IT, the Montreal SEO agency and managed IT practice. Book from their site. This page is a field note, not a sales form.
